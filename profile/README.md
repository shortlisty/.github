## Shortlisty — structures every venue detail from your files, then turns it into a salesroom in minutes

Venue intelligence and client proposals for event agencies. Two connected layers on one platform: a searchable venue knowledge base built from the files you already have, and an interactive client salesroom generated from it — no PDF attachments, no email back-and-forth.

### Layer 1 — Venue Intelligence Platform

- **No manual data entry** — drop in venue decks, PDFs, floor plans and spec sheets; Shortlisty reads them and structures capacity, catering policy, AV specs, restrictions, contacts and pricing automatically.
- **Natural-language search** — "rooftop for 80 guests, kosher catering, no curfew" returns the right venues instantly; structured filters work alongside it.
- **Knowledge that stays** — when a planner leaves, their venue expertise stays in the shared library instead of their inbox.
- **Team-wide from day one** — new hires see the full portfolio immediately, with the source document behind every field.

### Layer 2 — Digital Sales Room for Events

- **Brief to pitch in minutes** — pick venues from the library and generate a branded pitch page in one click.
- **No client login** — your client opens one link on any device and sees photos, floor plans and the details relevant to their event.
- **Questions in context** — comments and answers stay attached to the venue they concern, not scattered across email threads.
- **Immutable approval record** — on approval, every field, file and decision is locked with a timestamp and its source. No disputes about what was agreed.

### How it works

1. **Upload** your venue files — the library is structured automatically.
2. **Search** the whole portfolio in plain language or filters.
3. **Pick** venues for the brief and send one branded link.
4. **Approve** — the client browses, comments and confirms, leaving a locked record.

### Who it's for

Agency teams, corporate event leads and solo planners on one side; anyone commissioning an event — corporate buyers, couples, team leads — on the other. First venue library up in minutes.
